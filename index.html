Welcome to Church4All

An Interdenominational
Fellowship in Harare.

A place to love each other as God’s children and walk with Him in faith, truth, and genuine liberty.

Church4All Zimbabwe is built on unity, scripture, and mutual love. Whether you have walked with Christ for decades or are seeking answers for the first time, you are welcomed here with open arms.

Every Sunday @ 2:00 PM Gather with believers from across the city.

What Makes Us
Who We Are.

Anchored in Ephesians 4:4 and committed to walking as one body in Christ.

01

Learn God’s Word

A place to sit and learn the word of God with clarity, depth, and practical life application for your daily walk.

Love One Another

A place to love each other as God’s children — fostering an authentic, caring family where everyone belongs.

A place for worshipping in true liberty — lifting high the name of Jesus with sincere, heartfelt praise.
