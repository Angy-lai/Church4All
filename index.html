Our heart

A faith that moves
with love.

We believe church is not just a place we go. It is the people we become when we show up for one another and for our neighbors.

Our mandate is simple: make room for people to encounter God, find belonging, and put faith into action in the everyday.

01 / VISION

Hope for every home.

To see people and communities transformed by the hope of Jesus, with a place of belonging for every generation.

02 / MISSION

Love people well.

We gather to worship, grow through the Word, and serve our city with open hands and generous hearts.

03 / MANDATE

Make room for all.

We welcome every person, nurture authentic faith, and equip each other to carry kindness beyond our walls.
